# Related Work

Employee scheduling and workforce management have been the focus of both academic research and industry solutions. Traditional approaches often rely on manual processes or basic CRUD interfaces, lacking advanced analytics and AI integration. Recent developments in scheduling software have introduced modular UI components, RESTful APIs, and limited automation, but few offer a unified, extensible, and AI-ready architecture as demonstrated by Schichtplan.

Notable related work includes:
- **Open-source scheduling platforms** (e.g., ERPNext, Odoo): Provide basic scheduling and management but lack deep AI integration and modern UI paradigms.
- **Academic research on AI-driven scheduling**: Focuses on optimization algorithms and predictive analytics, but often lacks user-centric, extensible interfaces and full-stack integration.
- **Commercial workforce management suites**: Offer advanced features but are typically closed-source, less customizable, and may not expose modular components for integration.

Schichtplan bridges these gaps by combining a modern, customizable UI with a backend-agnostic, AI-ready architecture, setting a new standard for intelligent scheduling platforms.
